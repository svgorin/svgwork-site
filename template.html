info Setup Instructions

Click on any region header to expand/collapse the nodes. Click the copy button next to a node to copy its configuration link. Then open your client application (e.g., v2RayTun, Hiddify, Karing, Incy, or Rabbit hole) and choose "Import config from clipboard" or add a new profile.
